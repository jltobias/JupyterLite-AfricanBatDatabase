### How to interpret these studies

- Habitat-suitability models identify areas that may support bat species; they do not detect Ebola virus in bats or demonstrate transmission.
- Detection of antibodies indicates prior exposure to related viral antigens; by itself, it does not demonstrate that a bat was shedding infectious virus or transmitting it.
- The proposed exposure pathway discussed by Marí Saéz et al. concerns a specific index-case investigation and is not direct observation of transmission from an identified bat.
- The studies do not establish that all bat species carry Ebola virus. The StoryMap's mapped localities are occurrence records, not confirmed roosts or pathogen detections.
- A bat record within 50 km of a mine is not evidence of mine impact, infection, human exposure, or disease risk.
