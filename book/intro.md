# African bats & mineral facilities

This project explores the spatial relationship between bat occurrence records and mineral locations across Africa. It combines the African Bat Database point shapefile supplied for this project, the U.S. Geological Survey's 2006 mineral-facility inventory, its selected 2017 critical-mineral point layer, and a separately attributed supplemental location for Kibali Gold Mine.

The goal is an auditable location-by-location story—not a claim that proximity proves ecological impact or disease risk. The 2017 USGS source includes mines, deposits, districts, and regions but does not classify the individual features, so the map labels them as unclassified resource features. The interactive map separates location-to-record distance from optional buffers centered on individual bat records.

```{admonition} Read the map carefully
:class: warning

The supplied bat shapefile contains occurrence localities, not a field identifying a confirmed roost. Unless a location is independently verified, this project calls it a **bat record**. A record inside a distance band is not evidence that bats roost at, forage in, or are affected by a mine.
```

## Story structure

The story advances from facility to facility. Each chapter pairs the selected location's recorded source, commodity, type, and available characteristics with nearby bat records and their source references. Species photos and facility photos are individually attributed; a species illustration is not presented as a photograph of the animal at that specific site.
