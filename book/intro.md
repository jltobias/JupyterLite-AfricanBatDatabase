# African bats & mineral facilities

This project explores the spatial relationship between bat occurrence records and mineral facilities across Africa. It combines the African Bat Database point shapefile supplied for this project with the U.S. Geological Survey's 2006 mineral-facility inventory.

The goal is an auditable, mine-by-mine story—not a claim that proximity proves ecological impact or disease risk. The interactive map separates facility-to-record distance from optional buffers centered on individual bat records.

```{admonition} Read the map carefully
:class: warning

The supplied bat shapefile contains occurrence localities, not a field identifying a confirmed roost. Unless a location is independently verified, this project calls it a **bat record**. A record inside a distance band is not evidence that bats roost at, forage in, or are affected by a mine.
```

## Story structure

The story advances from facility to facility. Each chapter pairs the facility's recorded commodity, type, status, mining method, operator, and capacity with the nearby bat records and their source references. Species photos and facility photos are individually attributed; a species illustration is not presented as a photograph of the animal at that specific site.
