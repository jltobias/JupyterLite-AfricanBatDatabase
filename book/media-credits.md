The StoryMap stores thumbnail images locally and shows the creator credit, source page, and license for each image. Some images are representatives of the stated genus or are illustrative for records marked `cf.`; see the image note for those cases. A representative image is not evidence that the pictured animal was recorded at that locality. *Plerotes anchietae* currently has no verified reusable photograph and uses a placeholder in the StoryMap and PDF. Follow each image's individual license; several iNaturalist images are licensed CC BY-NC and are not licensed for commercial reuse.
